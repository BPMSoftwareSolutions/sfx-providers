# Circuit-led redesign: engineering, evidence, and collaboration

Use one recurring **illustrated circuit language** across these slides: a translucent inference prism emits an amber proposal packet; the packet reaches a cyan capability circuit; a keyed violet binding socket connects a coral provider actuator; a narrow silver trace carries evidence back. The drawing is an architectural analogy, not physical circuitry. Show meaningful gates, branches, boundaries, and outcomes rather than decorative circuit patterns.

Make the main illustration occupy about two-thirds of each slide. Use saturated cyan, violet, amber, and coral against deep ink backgrounds, with warm light backgrounds for the reference atlas. Render dimensional components with clear silhouettes and restrained lighting. Keep all labels, source links, dates, and status text native/editable over the artwork. Avoid badges, shields, institutional logos, handshakes, and green certification seals.

**State grammar:** solid lit routes = the mechanism or recorded route being explained; interrupted route = absent business path; dotted route = proposed work; a numbered witness tab = retained evidence. Always label the state in words as well as color. A glowing line alone must never imply a proved safety guarantee.
